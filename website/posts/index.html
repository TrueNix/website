The Private Key Was 4: Naming the Eight Certificates Go Distrusted

Mozilla confirmed on 20 October 2026 that private keys for eight eMudhra intermediate CA certificates are compromised, and the public disclosure states the cause: the CA generated them incorrectly, so anyone can recover them from the certificates.
