CVE-2026-94603 reached NVD on 13 October 2026 at CVSS 10.0: any OCI image carrying the io.podman.annotations.checkpoint.runtime.name annotation made podman run silently discard the operator’s own sandbox flags, including --cap-drop=ALL.
